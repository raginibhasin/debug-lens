ACCOUNT RECOVERY

Get back into
your workspace.

For security, password resets are run locally by the workspace owner. DebugLens does not email a reset link or expose account recovery over the public web.

Only someone with local server access can reset an account.

SELF-HOSTED RECOVERY

Reset your password.

Ask the workspace owner to run this from the DebugLens project folder.

python -m flask --app app reset-account-password

The owner enters the account email and sets a new password of at least 12 characters. Password entry is hidden in the terminal. Then return here and sign in with the new password.
